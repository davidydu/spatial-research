## Independent R010 challenge

Read the complete memory/DSE proposal against R006, R008, compiler/state contracts and roadmap. No additional unresolved architectural contradiction was found within that inspected scope. The decisive cases remain proposed acceptance tests, not observed hardware results:

- Copying `[1,2,3]` into positions 1–3 of the same backing `[1,2,3,4]` must give `[1,1,2,3]`; tile-local staging or a forward copy is insufficient. R010 preserves full selected-value snapshot semantics or requires an equivalent dependence proof.
- Publication capacity two cannot become three credits because a candidate adds physical storage. Stop admission, allocator policy and numeric topology likewise retain semantic identity; changing them creates a separate checked profile.
- A finite DMA adapter cannot admit every request before draining merely because unlimited C simulation completes. Bounded issue, response completion and named progress assumptions are required.
- Unknown aliases/port assignment/progress or failed vendor hard constraints cannot enter the feasible DSE set; cost estimates and solver timeout do not prove legality.

Direct pinned source spot-checks supported three consequential claims: original SRAM1 rejects nonconstant/nonparameter sizes (`spatial@e7a8f2f:src/spatial/lang/SRAM.scala:139-142`); allocator status can change on request before response (`spatial@e7a8f2f:fringe/src/fringe/templates/memory/DRAMAllocator.scala:61-88`); per-point DSE validity uses capacity/errors while several reruns are commented out (`spatial@e7a8f2f:src/spatial/dse/DSEThread.scala:109-150`). The corresponding Python contracts are deliberate redesigns, not inherited guarantees.

Independently retrieved [AMD UG1399 2025.2 manual-burst guidance](https://docs.amd.com/r/2025.2-English/ug1399-vitis-hls/Using-Manual-Burst?contentId=GZ~rctvGB5Rk7_ULx_dNIw), accessed 2026-09-30: its C-simulation and synthesis-limit sections give different cross-process guidance. R010 correctly retains single-owner sequencing and demands release-specific executable evidence before accepting a split route. [Storage binding documentation](https://docs.amd.com/r/2025.2-English/ug1399-vitis-hls/pragma-HLS-bind_storage), accessed the same date, supports explicit memory type/implementation/latency compatibility rather than a generic “dual-port” assumption. Neither retrieval ran a vendor tool.

## What must close now, and what follows adoption

**Research closure:** independent reviews, manager acceptance, and synthesis synchronization are complete. R12 remains open only for final integrated validation and publication of this package. Reviewers retained concrete counterexamples rather than replacing evidence with a consensus statement.

**Future implementation evidence:** exact numeric library/certificates, canonical schema/import checks, nested/task snapshot scaling, independent conformance/performance, concrete allocator/component/ABI routes, finite-capacity progress, numeric vendor wrappers and physical timing. These are honest gates in the saved design. They do not become passed experiments when a research recommendation is accepted.

The strongest objection remains the breadth of the custom semantic/protocol/hardware contract. The framework probe cannot establish its scalability, and finite fixtures cannot establish universal concurrent refinement. The roadmap's family-by-family gates make that risk reviewable; they do not remove it. This audit distinguishes research acceptance from architecture adoption and executable support; publication closure is recorded below.
